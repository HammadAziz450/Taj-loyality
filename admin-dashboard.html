Rewards:

Our monthly reward program is specially designed for Site Managers (SM), Supervisors (SS), and Head Cashiers as an appreciation for their performance and loyalty. At the end of each month, eligible members receive exclusive discounts on selected products such as shirts, medical items, soaps, and other daily-use essentials. These rewards are provided as discount benefits only and do not include free products. The program encourages consistent performance while helping staff enjoy valuable savings on quality products.
